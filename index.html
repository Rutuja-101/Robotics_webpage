About Us

A community of passionate students building the future, one robot at a time.

The Robotics Club is a student-led community dedicated to learning and experimenting with robotics, electronics, and automation. We provide a hands-on environment where members can develop practical skills beyond the classroom.

Whether you're a beginner curious about Arduino or an experienced programmer looking to build autonomous systems, our club welcomes all skill levels. Together, we learn, collaborate, and compete.
